# OmniServe AI: 100 Workflow Purposes

This guide explains what every OmniServe workflow is for and how it contributes to the connected company operating system. All workflows share the Supabase Customer 360, n8n orchestration, audit logging, risk controls, and human approval when required.

| # | Workflow | Purpose and practical use |
|---:|---|---|

| 7 | SLA Assignment | Assign the correct response and resolution deadline for the customer and issue. |
| 8 | Case Creation | Create a structured support case with a case number, owner, department, priority, and status. |
| 9 | Duplicate Detection | Find existing cases for the same issue before creating unnecessary duplicate work. |
| 10 | Department Routing | Send each request to Sales, Support, Billing, Service Delivery, Finance, or another team. |

| 12 | Knowledge Retrieval | Search documentation, FAQs, policies, and previous solutions for relevant answers. |
| 13 | Human Escalation | Transfer complex, sensitive, high-risk, or low-confidence cases to an employee. |

## Combined purpose

Together, these workflows create a connected AI-assisted company in which every customer interaction can be received, understood, routed, acted on, audited, measured, and improved. Automation handles repeatable work, while people retain control of financial, legal, security, privacy, and other high-risk decisions.
